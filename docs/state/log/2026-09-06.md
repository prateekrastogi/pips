## `PitStore.as_of` in one pass

The method priced itself in its docstring — O(keys × records), ~0.4 s on the real master, "the fix is
an index by key, not a cache of the answer" — and the daily panel build asks it once per trading day
(~745 times, ~5 minutes of every build) while the prerank's explainer asks the corporate-actions
table once per overnight pair. Rewritten as the prescribed index: one loop over the records, both
filters BEFORE choosing, latest `known_from` wins, a tie goes to the later write (file order, `>=`),
sorted by key, `CLOSE` dropped. `_latest_version` stays for the append path.
